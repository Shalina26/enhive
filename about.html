Our story

It all started with a simple weekend dilemma – finding the perfect activity to enjoy with friends and family. Back in 2018, our founders were tired of spending hours searching different websites and making countless phone calls just to plan a fun day out. There had to be a better way.

That's when enhive was born. We envisioned a single platform where people could discover, compare, and book amazing leisure experiences with just a few clicks. What began as a small team working from a garage has now grown into the region's most trusted leisure booking platform.

Today, we partner with over 500 venues – from bowling alleys and escape rooms to adventure parks and mini golf courses. Our mission remains simple: to make planning your next great adventure as exciting as the adventure itself. We believe life's best moments happen when we step away from our screens and dive into real experiences with the people we care about.

Ready to discover your next adventure? Let's make it happen.

Our mission

At enhive, our mission is to transform how people discover and experience leisure activities. We exist to eliminate the friction between wanting to have fun and actually having it – turning the tedious task of planning into an exciting journey of discovery.

We believe that every person deserves easy access to memorable experiences that enrich their lives, strengthen relationships, and create lasting memories. By connecting adventurers with incredible venues and activities in their community, we're not just facilitating bookings – we're fostering connections, supporting local businesses, and helping people live more fulfilling lives.

Our commitment extends beyond convenience. We strive to be the bridge that brings communities together, one booking at a time. When you choose LeisureLink, you're not just planning an activity – you're investing in local businesses, creating jobs, and contributing to a vibrant leisure ecosystem that benefits everyone.

We're here to make every adventure accessible, every booking effortless, and every experience unforgettable.
